# Round 5 — Any Niche, Proven Money: Criteria & Method

> Date: 2026-09-24. Rounds 1–4 searched only B2B back-office / workflow niches (~194 candidates, 0 survivors).
> Founder decision (2026-09-24): **no niche restriction.** Any legal niche is in scope: B2C, creators, apps, templates, extensions, games, content/SEO, directories, data, adult, dating, gambling-adjacent, "useless" products. **No moral filter.** AI wrappers are allowed. Goal: money, solo developer, €1k+ MRR, minimal effort.
> Fixed before searching.

## Core question

> **"Where do several independent solo (or 1–2 person) operators ALREADY make ≥ $1k/month from a low-effort product, and why could a newcomer still get customers there?"**

Profit can't be guaranteed. The proxy is **replication of verified money**, not predicted demand.

## Gates (all must pass)

| # | Gate | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Proven money** | ≥ 2 independent solo/small operators in the niche with **verified or credibly disclosed** revenue ≥ $1k/month (TrustMRR, Acquire/Microns/Flippa listings with P&L, Stripe-verified Indie Hackers, public open-startup dashboards, interviews with figures). URL required. |
| G2 | **Newcomer can win customers** | A concrete distribution gap: a language/geo gap (e.g. PT/ES), an under-served sub-segment, SEO keywords with weak results, a platform marketplace with low competition, or a price gap. "Build it better" does not count. |
| G3 | **Solo-buildable** | One developer, MVP ≤ ~8 weeks part-time. |
| G4 | **Minimal effort** | ≤ ~5 h/week ops after launch (support, content, moderation). No sales calls. |
| G5 | **Low capital** | ≤ ~€100/month running cost before revenue (was €35; relaxed only if clearly justified, flag it). |
| G6 | **Factual feasibility** (not moral) | Legal for a Brazil resident to operate. A payment processor will actually accept it (adult, gambling and dating have processor limits). No licence the founder can't get. Platform terms-of-service risk stated. |

## Kill rules

- K-R5-1: all proven earners rely on an audience/brand/network the founder lacks (e.g. a creator's followers).
- K-R5-2: money came from a one-time trend that has already collapsed.
- K-R5-3: the niche is dominated by funded players and the solo earners are all pre-2022 SEO survivors with no new entrants succeeding.
- K-R5-4: needs heavy paid acquisition (CAC > 3 months of revenue) with no organic channel.
- K-R5-5: G6 fails (no processor, licence needed, or illegal in Brazil).

## Output per survivor

1. Proven earners (names, revenue, source URL, age).
2. Distribution gap and the channel a newcomer would use.
3. MVP scope, build weeks, monthly cost, ops h/week.
4. Price and customers needed for €1k MRR.
5. Top 3 risks.

## Workstreams

- **R5-A** Verified-revenue sources: TrustMRR, Acquire.com, Microns, Flippa, Indie Hackers, Starter Story, open-startup dashboards. Cluster niches with multiple solo earners.
- **R5-B** Consumer & platform ecosystems: mobile subscription apps, Chrome extensions, Shopify/WordPress/Figma/Notion plugins & templates, Etsy/Gumroad digital products, Discord/Telegram bots, games.
- **R5-C** Adult & stigmatised-but-legal: adult creator tools, AI companion/chat, adult content tooling, dating, astrology/tarot, betting tips/tools, crypto tools. Include processor/legal facts (G6).
- **R5-D** "Boring/useless" & content: programmatic SEO sites, niche directories with paid listings, job boards, data/API products, lead lists, newsletters, generators/calculators, domain/parked plays.
