# Round 4 — Paid-Labour Search, Done-for-You Allowed: Criteria & Method

> Date: 2026-09-23. Rounds 1–3: NO-GO (see `research/R1..R3-SYNTHESIS.md`).
> Founder decisions (2026-09-23): **A** rerun R3 properly (full search quota, plus a manual sweep of sites that block bots); **B** relax the human-share constraint so done-for-you services with a human share above 10% are allowed.
> These rules are **fixed before searching**.

## Why this round

R3 pattern #4: buyers repeatedly pay humans **even when cheap tools exist** (transaction coordinators $300–700/file vs ListedKit $15; insurance-agency VA teams $3,449/mo vs free certificate tools). They pay for responsibility being taken off their plate. R4 tests whether a **software-leveraged done-for-you service** can capture that spend within the founder's capacity.

## Capacity model (hard)

- Founder service capacity: **~10 h/week ≈ 43 h/month** at ~€1k MRR (founder decision).
- Price band widened: **€100–500/month per customer** (done-for-you prices against human providers, not tools).
- Constraint: **customers × human hours per customer ≤ 43 h/month** at the target MRR.

| Customers × price | Max human time per customer per month | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|
| 2 × €500 | ~21 h | ~€23/h |
| 5 × €200 | ~8.6 h | ~€23/h |
| 10 × €100 | ~4.3 h | ~€23/h |

- **Leverage test:** software must cut human time by at least 50% compared with how today's human provider does the job. Otherwise the founder is just a freelancer competing with offshore rates of $2–9/hr (R3 evidence).
