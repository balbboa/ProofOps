# Round 3 — Bottom-Up Search from Paid Repetitive Labour: Criteria & Method

> Date: 2026-09-23. Rounds 1–2: NO-GO (see `research/R1-SYNTHESIS.md`, `research/R2-SYNTHESIS.md`).
> These rules are **fixed before searching** and must not be changed mid-round.

## Core question

> **"What exact repetitive task are businesses currently paying €X to a human/outsourcer to perform repeatedly, where the deliverable itself could be generated by software?"**

Order of evidence (mandatory):
1. **Paid labour first.** A recurring, priced transaction to a human (freelancer gig, agency/BPO/VA price list, per-file fee, job posting for a repetitive role), with a price and a frequency.
2. **Then the deliverable.** Software can *generate the output* (the filing, the report, the listing, the document), not merely check it.
3. **Then the competition.** Does software already do it cheaply and self-serve?

## Kill criteria

R2's K1–K11 still apply (see `R2-CRITERIA.md`), with K6 read as "requires sales calls or demos". Added:

- **K12.** The paid value is actually human judgement, responsibility, licensed sign-off, negotiation or relationships, not the deliverable.
- **K13.** The deliverable must be entered into a proprietary system with no API/import path (software can't complete it).
- **K14.** The human share can't plausibly fall to ≤ ~30 min per customer per month at 20 customers.
- **K15.** The spend is one-off or rarer than monthly per customer.

## Economics target

Can 5–30 customers at €50–200/month (≈ R$300–1,200/month) plausibly reach ~€1k MRR, with each customer replacing spend of at least roughly 2–5× the price?

## Funnel

- **Stage A:** harvest paid-labour transactions from marketplaces, service menus, job postings (global + Brazil).
- **Stage B:** per transaction: price, frequency, buyer, deliverable, standardisability.
- **Stage C:** kill with K1–K15.
- **Stage D:** deep-dive survivors only.
