# R5-D — "Boring/useless" products, content and data niches

> Date: 2026-09-24. Rulebook: `R5-CRITERIA.md` (gates G1–G6, kill rules K-R5-1..5, output per survivor).
> Workstream: programmatic SEO, directories, job boards, data/API, lead lists, newsletters, generators/calculators, AI image generators, domains, novelty products, print-on-demand, Brazil-specific plays.
> Method: desk research only. ~107 web searches plus ~20 page fetches.
> Labels: **VERIFIED** = revenue pulled from Stripe/RevenueCat by TrustMRR. **SELF-REPORTED** = founder's own claim in an interview or post. **ESTIMATE** = third-party guess (Latka, SimilarWeb). **UNVERIFIED** = claim with no traceable source.

## §2 Per-candidate notes

**#1–3 Ad-supported SEO (pSEO, calculators, tool sites).**
- Every source checked points the same way:
  - Global publishers lost about a third of Google traffic in 2025 ([Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/media-audience-and-business-data/google-traffic-down-2025-trends-report-2026/)).
  - Utility content (weather, TV guides, horoscopes, the same class as converters) was named as the most affected.
  - Zero-click searches are now around 60%, and 68% in Brazil in early 2026 ([Tropk](https://www.tropk.ai/google-ai-overviews-brasil-impacto-trafego/)).
- The pSEO "success" stories found are sold by pSEO-tool vendors and agencies, with no verifiable P&L.
- Flippa shows what a small calculator site really earns: $45–$728/mo. Even at 25k pageviews with Mediavine, the peak was $728.
- An indie developer's 100k-visit site dropped from $800 to $200/mo on AdSense in 2024 ([IH](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/from-200-to-3k-month-why-i-ditched-adsense-for-direct-ad-sales-with-numbers-d1098e3aae)).
- AdSense is also a single point of failure.
- **PT makes this worse, not better:**
  - RPM is 30–50% lower than English for the same niche.
  - Brazil got AI Overviews 3 months after the US and AI Mode 6 months after.
  - PT SERPs are held by 4devs (3M visits/mo), Serasa and banks. Solo PT sites like nagringa.dev and meuimposto2026 already rank for tax/salary calculators.

**#4 Vertical directories.**
- Property Vendors is the one clean data point: a verified ~$2k in the last 30 days in "property preservation" contractors, running since 2018. That is the "boring" pattern at its best.
- Frey Chu's $2.5k/mo is self-reported by a course seller.
- The channel is still SEO, so the #1 problems apply. Businesses only pay for featured listings once the directory already ranks, and early revenue needs cold email to vendors.
- Kept at UNCERTAIN, not KILL, only because the vertical-B2B version (job board plus vendor directory) has verified money.

**#5 Launch/submission directories.** This market is real, with verified earners. But it sells visibility to indie founders, and the sellers reach them through their own X audiences. That is K-R5-1. A Brazilian version would be tiny.

**#6 Employer-paid niche job boards.**
- Solid G1: two verified earners (Property Vendors, JobBoardSearch) plus self-reported MoAIJobs (2023) and Work With Indies. MoAIJobs shows a post-2022 entrant can still succeed.
- The pattern: catch a niche early (AI jobs in 2023), fill it with scraped/aggregated listings, then charge $99–299 per featured post.
- The G2 problem: the gap is "find the next rising niche". That is a bet, not a gap we can show today.
- Brazil does not help. Employers post free on Gupy, LinkedIn and Indeed. Niche PT boards already exist (Farmavagas, Trampos, Remotar), and R$49.90/mo is the market price for posting.

**#7 Seeker-paid ("flipped") boards.**
- The most interesting model found: scrape ATS jobs (Greenhouse/Lever/Workable), then paywall search at $5/week or $18–20/month.
- Two self-reported earners: CareerHound $12k MRR and Remote Rocketship $6k MRR in 2023. The Latka estimate for Remote Rocketship is much lower, so treat its figure with care.
- Distribution is the whole game:
  - CareerHound pays older-demographic UGC creators under $20 per video and lives with 31% monthly churn.
  - Remote Rocketship's traffic started with one viral Reddit post.
- Neither is a gap a newcomer can reuse on demand, and copies are now live.

**#8 Flipped board in PT.** Tested honestly, and it fails:
- "Vagas na gringa" already has #jobnagringa (R$50/mo), NaGringa (on TrustMRR) and vaganagringa.dev.
- Domestic remote jobs are free on Remotar, and Gupy exposes a free public job API and portal. A paywall over free data does not convert.
- Brazilian candidates already know paid tiers (Catho, BNE), so the idea is not new to them. It is just taken.

**#9 Apify/RapidAPI actors.**
- The platform total is real: $1.4M/mo paid out. But the average is about $470 per developer, and no named solo developer's earnings could be found. That fails G1 on evidence.
- Maintenance is 5–10 h per actor per quarter, plus public failure ratings.
- The PT gap is not there: Zap/VivaReal/OLX and Gupy already have 4–10 actors each.
- It would need Apify's own developer-earnings data or interviews to move up.

**#10–11 Brazil public data (CNPJ/CEP/B3) and CNPJ lead lists.** This is the most crowded PT niche found. The Receita Federal open dataset is resold at R$0.01 per CNPJ, and free APIs exist for every lookup. The only verified BR data API (Bolsai) is at $624 MRR.

**#12 Local-lead extraction SaaS.**
- Strong G1: two solo launches from 2026, both verified at $4–9k in the last 30 days. So K-R5-3 does not apply.
- But there is no named distribution gap:
  - Outscraper, scrap.io and Apify serve the same buyers globally.
  - Brazil has a long tail of Hotmart extractors and CNPJ tools.
- G6 risks: scraping Google Maps breaks Google's terms. Enriching owner emails and phones raises LGPD/GDPR questions. Proxy and enrichment costs could break the €100/mo cap.
- localprospects' founder ran an agency and built for his own use, which suggests some network advantage.

**#13 Newsletters.** Money depends on audience, and writing plus sponsor sales take more than 5 h/week. "No-writing" automated newsletter income claims are UNVERIFIED Medium posts.

**#14–15 AI generators.**
- Headshots: winners are 2023-era founders with audiences. A 2025 US entrant verified at $60 in 30 days. Brazil already has at least two Pix-priced local products.
- Other generators: TrustMRR's long tail is mostly under $1k/mo, with a few $1.7–4k exceptions and no shared channel. Free general image models (ChatGPT, Gemini) keep eating the "magic box" value.

**#16 Personalised songs.** No verified solo revenue anywhere. In PT the price has already dropped to R$10–20 per song across at least 5 sellers, sold mostly through Meta ads.

**#17 Domain parking.** Dead. Google ended AdSense for Domains, and Team Internet's search segment fell 59%.

**#18 POD.** Declining and saturated. Refer to R5-B if it is pursued.

**#19 Vehicle-plate reports.** Many resellers exist, but none disclose revenue. The underlying DETRAN/RENAVAM data sourcing is legally unclear, so it fails G1 and has G6 risk.

**#20 Concursos.** Incumbents plus an AI-native low-price entrant (Gabaritei, R$49/yr) plus free alert apps. No gap.

## §4 Segment finding

1. **Ad-supported content is the wrong side of the AI shift.**
   - Every ad/SEO-monetised candidate (pSEO, calculators, tools, parking, concursos content) fails G1 or G2.
   - AI Overviews and AI Mode are live in Portuguese (Aug 2024 and Sep 2025).
   - Brazilian RPM is 30–50% lower than English.
   - Public P&Ls for small utility sites sit at $45–$728/mo.
2. **The Portuguese hypothesis fails for this segment.** In 10 PT tests (CNPJ/CEP data, lead lists, calculators, concursos, remote/international job boards, AI headshots, personalised songs, vehicle reports, Apify scrapers, directories), PT was **already served**, usually by several local players and often with Pix pricing. Brazil's open-data habit (Receita Federal, BrasilAPI, Gupy's public API) makes data products worse, because the raw data is free.
3. **Where verified money exists** (job boards, vertical directories, launch directories, lead-extraction SaaS), it comes from one of three things:
   - an early niche position (MoAIJobs, Property Vendors),
   - an existing audience (launch directories, HeadshotPro), or
   - paid or viral acquisition (flipped job boards).

   None of these is a structural gap a newcomer can plan for.
4. **One signal to pass to R5-B:** PT-only **consumer apps** can grow fast.
